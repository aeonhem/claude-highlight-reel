# CoD Highlight Reels: Audio Brief

Use this for every CoD multiplayer Short.

## Format: Shorts breakout style
- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, 60fps if the source allows.
- Length 15 to 35 seconds. The top CoD Short in our research (8M views) ran 35 seconds.
- Structure:
  1. **Tension open (0 to 6 s):** game audio only, no music and no added ambience.
  2. **The drop:** start the beat exactly on the first kill. This "music kicks in" moment is what makes the format work.
  3. **Body:** cut kills and movement on the beat. Hitmarkers and gunfire stay punchy over the music.
  4. **Ending:** close on the best kill or a team wipe, letting the music end on a hit or loop cleanly back to the start.

## Music: StreamBeats only
- Source: StreamBeats by Harris Heller. Download from streambeats.bandcamp.com by clicking "buy" and entering $0. Get WAV or FLAC.
- Licence (as of Sep 2026): free and perpetual. Monetised YouTube videos are allowed and no credit is required. Check the current terms yourself before relying on this.
- Rules: you can't remix, sample or re-upload tracks as music. Trimming, fading and syncing a track to video is normal sync use and is fine. Don't run tracks through AI tools.
- Genre picks: high-energy EDM, dubstep, drum and bass or hip-hop. For the hardest sound, pick tracks with a sharp drop in the first 10 seconds.
- If a Content ID claim appears anyway, use the StreamBeats False Claim Report form on streambeats.com.
- Optional: put "Music: StreamBeats" in the description. It isn't required but it helps.

## SFX: Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundle
- Source: sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc. The 2026 bundle is over 7 GB of 24-bit WAV, and older years are in the archive.
- Licence: royalty-free, commercial use allowed, no credit needed. The files can't be used to train AI models (editing video with them is fine).
- Use only two kinds of sound from it:
  - **Whooshes:** on transitions and leading into the drop.
  - **Bass booms:** on big kills and the drop.
- Nothing else from the bundle: no ambience, risers, impacts or foley. Extra audio pulls focus from the gameplay.

## Game audio
- Mute CoD's own music (killstreak themes, end-of-match tracks, menu music). It can trigger copyright claims.
- Keep gunfire, hitmarkers, footsteps and callouts.

## Mix targets
- Final loudness around -14 LUFS integrated with true peak at or below -1 dBTP. YouTube normalises to roughly -14, so louder masters just get turned down.
- Phone check: make sure the beat and hitmarkers still come through on phone speakers.

## Watch-outs
- YouTube may limit ads on montages where "gratuitous violence is the focal point". Titles that frame the clip around skill or gameplay ("1v4 clutch", "flow state") are safer than ones built around gore.
- Don't show cheats, glitches or exploits. Activision has struck those videos before.
